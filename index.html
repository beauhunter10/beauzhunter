About

Data-Driven Decision Making for the Real World

I believe the best analytics don't just measure performance — they change how organizations think. With a background spanning data engineering, business intelligence, and customer-facing leadership, I specialize in building the automated data infrastructure that powers S&OP and Integrated Business Planning.

My approach is simple: a number that moves in a direction tells you more than fourteen numbers that require a PhD to interpret. Automation doesn't scale output — it scales thinking. When your data pipeline runs itself, your team can finally focus on anticipating problems instead of reacting to them.

From Snowflake data warehouses to real-time dashboards, I've built the systems that give leadership the clarity to plan boldly and execute precisely.
